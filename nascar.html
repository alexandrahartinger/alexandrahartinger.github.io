NASCAR x Main Street Auto

Main Street Auto has officially partnered with NASCAR driver CJ McLaughlin, bringing speed, community, and creativity together on the track. This page documents that exciting collaboration—from design mockups to real-life race moments.

Meet CJ McLaughlin

CJ McLaughlin is a talented and determined NASCAR driver currently competing in the Xfinity Series. Known for his consistency, focus, and passion for the sport, CJ brings grit to every race. With Main Street Auto behind him, CJ’s journey on the track has gained a bold new look and even more horsepower. From suit design to vehicle wraps, every detail reflects our shared pursuit of excellence.
