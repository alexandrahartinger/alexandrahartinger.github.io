CJ McLaughlin x Main Street Auto

In 2024, Main Street Auto made its NASCAR debut by partnering with CJ McLaughlin and sponsoring the No. 92 Chevrolet Camaro in the Xfinity Series. This page celebrates that milestone partnership, featuring design assets, creative direction, and branding artwork developed to bring our vision to the track.
