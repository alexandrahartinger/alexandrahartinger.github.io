01 / Social-first storytelling

Real people.

Auto repair is personal. I wanted our social channels to reflect the people behind the counter, the expertise in the shop, and the communities we serve.

As the sole in-house creative when I started, I owned the process from idea to final post — building a content calendar, shooting and editing video, designing assets, and publishing across four platforms.

As I stepped into my Marketing Manager role, I guided a design team from creative brief to final campaign assets, maintaining a unified visual language across 95+ locations.
