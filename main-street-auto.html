Main Street Auto

Our Story

Main Street Auto is a 100+ location auto repair network built on small-town values, delivering honest, reliable car care across the Southeast. It combines the trust of locally owned shops with the strength and resources of a larger brand to better serve its communities.

Our Parent Company

Northern Rock

A parent brand focused on purpose-driven strategy and design — built to support brands like Main Street Auto from the ground up.

Rebrand Overview

Northern Rock is the parent company of Main Street Auto and leads with a clean, purpose-driven design approach. The brand is all about intention — muted colors, and simplicity. When I developed Main Street Auto’s look, I went the opposite direction on purpose. It’s loud and local — bold reds, rough textures, and straightforward type that speak the language of small towns, shop talk, and real people.
