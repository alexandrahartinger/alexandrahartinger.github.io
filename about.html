Storyteller.

Strategist.

I’ve always been the person who translates what others can’t quite put into words. I dig into the "why" behind what we love, what we hate, and what we need—then I turn those messy human truths into a clear strategy.

My first real-world shift into creative strategy was leading the SCADpro David Yurman Holiday 2023 campaign. Being at the center of a production that massive was a unique experience that taught me how to take a client’s high-level dream and turn it into a tangible reality.

After SCAD, I moved back to Atlanta and joined Main Street Auto. I jumped in as their only designer to build the brand from the ground up, translating their potential into a professional image that aligned with the multi-million dollar company they were becoming.

Right now, I’m a partner at Sole 1, building a device for people who suffer from foot drop—something I live with every day. I bridge the gap between engineers and the community, translating technical specs into real-life solutions. Connecting with others and giving them a voice has been the most rewarding work of my career.

Since then, I’ve relocated to the beautiful state of Colorado. I'm ready for the next chapter and the next story that needs telling.
